Pros
  • Recurring revenue without asking again; students don't forget to renew
  • Industry standard; easy to compare and price later (yearly plan = one more plan)
  • Fits "Premium" as an ongoing service
Cons
  • More to build: subscription states (active, pending, halted, cancelled), retries, a cancel button, billing page; ~4–5 days
  • Webhooks must be right or people lose access they paid for (or keep it free)
  • UPI Autopay mandates are a step many students distrust or abandon; some banks/apps decline them
  • Razorpay Subscriptions may need a separate activation after KYC
  • Refund and "I forgot to cancel" support load
